In this section, we will look at important German subordinating conjunctions.

da because, since; usually introduces information already known to the listener; a subordinate clause with da usually stands first

Da ich diesen Film bereits gesehen habe, gehe ich lieber einkaufen. — Since I have already seen this film, I would rather go shopping.

seit/seitdem since; expresses an action that started in the past and has not ended yet.

Seitdem (seit) ich in Deutschland lebe, lerne ich Deutsch. — Since I have been living in Germany, I have been learning German.

bis until; expresses the point up to which an action continues

Bis du mit der Arbeit fertig bist, sehe ich ein bisschen fern. — Until you finish the work, I will watch a little TV.

indem by, by doing; expresses the way an action is carried out

Man kann am besten abnehmen, indem man Sport treibt und nicht so viel isst. — The best way to lose weight is by doing sports and not eating so much. In other words, the goal is to lose weight. How do we achieve it? By doing sports and not eating much.
