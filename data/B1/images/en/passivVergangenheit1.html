Passive in the past

Passiv can express either an action or a state:

Vorgangspassiv / werden-Passiv
(action or process) 		Zustandspassiv / sein-Passiv
(result of an action or process)
Passiv in Präteritum
werden in Präteritum + Partizip II of the main verb sein in Präteritum + Partizip II of the main verb
Das Haus wurde gebaut. — The house was built.
Die Probleme wurden gelöst. — The problems were solved. 		Das Haus war gebaut. — The house was built / was in a built state.
Die Probleme waren gelöst. — The problems were solved / were in a solved state.
Passiv in Perfekt (spoken past)
sein + Partizip II of the main verb + worden sein + Partizip II of the main verb + gewesen
Das Haus ist gebaut worden. — The house has been built.
Die Probleme sind gelöst worden. — The problems have been solved. 		Das Haus ist gebaut gewesen. — The house has been built / was in a built state.
Die Probleme sind gelöst gewesen. — The problems have been solved / were in a solved state.
