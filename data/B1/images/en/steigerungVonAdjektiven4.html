If we want to say that the object described by the Superlativ is one of several, we use the construction einer/eines/eine der _____sten (Genitiv Plural):

Das ist eines der schönsten Bilder in der Galerie. — This is one of the most beautiful pictures in the gallery.

Wir sind in einer der schönsten Städte Europas. — We are in one of the most beautiful cities in Europe.

In this case only einer/eines/eine are declined:
