Artikelwörter are words that stand before a noun and behave like an article. They differ in that after some of them the adjective declines weakly (as after the definite article), and after others strongly (as with the zero article).

trigger weak declension

jeder, -es, -e (each, every)

jeder gute Mensch

dieser, -es, -e (this)

dieser gute Mensch

jener, -es, -e (that)

jener gute Mensch

welcher, -es, -e (which)

welcher gute Mensch

mancher, -es, -e (some, a certain)

manche guten Menschen

solcher, -es, -e (such)

solche guten Menschen

alle (all)

alle guten Menschen

beide (both)

beide guten Menschen

sämtliche (all, every)

sämtliche guten Menschen

trigger strong declension

wenige (few)

wenige gute Menschen

einige (some, more than wenige)

einige gute Menschen

mehrere (several, more than einige)

mehrere gute Menschen

etliche (quite a few, some)

etliche gute Menschen

viele (many)

viele gute Menschen

Remember: viel and wenig are not declined when used with a singular noun.

Compare:

Ich habe viel/wenig Zeit. — I have much/little time.

Ich habe viele/wenige gute Bücher. — I have many/few good books.

Exception: Vielen Dank. — Thank you very much.
