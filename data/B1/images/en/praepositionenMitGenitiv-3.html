Instrumentale Präpositionen — means prepositions

anhand with the help of, by means of

Der Täter konnte anhand eines Fotos identifiziert werden. — The perpetrator could be identified by means of a photo.

mithilfe with the help of

Mithilfe seines Anwalts konnte sein Geld gerettet werden. — With the help of his lawyer, his money could be saved.

Finale Präpositionen — purpose prepositions

zwecks for the purpose of

Zwecks weiterer Befragungen wurde er zur nächsten Polizeistation mitgenommen. — For the purpose of further questioning, he was taken to the nearest police station.

It should be noted that prepositions with Genitiv are often used in formal speech, while spoken German tends to replace the genitive with the dative. Thus, after wegen and laut, Dativ can be used in conversation, and some prepositions allow von after them, in which case the noun also stands in Dativ: anhand von Fotos — with the help of photos, innerhalb von einem Jahr — within one year.
