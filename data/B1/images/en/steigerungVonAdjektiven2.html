Adjectives in the comparative degree (Komparativ) are declined in the same way as adjectives in the positive degree (Positiv) and have the form:

adjective + -er- + ending

Lea ist eine fleißige Schülerin. — Lea is a hardworking schoolgirl.

Beate ist eine fleißigere Schülerin. — Beate is a more hardworking schoolgirl.

Ihr älterer Bruder studiert in Wien. — Her older brother studies in Vienna.

The same rule also applies to special adjective forms:

In Chili gibt es guten Wein. — There is good wine in Chile.

In Italien gibt es einen besseren Wein. — There is better wine in Italy.

Declined adjectives in Komparativ can be used with zero, definite and indefinite articles, as well as their substitutes (words that behave like articles).
