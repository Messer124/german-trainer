The German verb brauchen is common in speech and is used in two ways: as an ordinary verb and as a modal-like verb.

As an ordinary verb, brauchen (+ Akk.) means “to need” and is a synonym of the verb benötigen.

Ich brauche deine Hilfe. — I need your help / I need your assistance.

Das Kind braucht neue Schuhe. — The child needs new shoes.

As a modal-like verb, brauchen forms the constructions brauchen nicht(kein) + zu + Infinitiv or brauchen nur(bloß)* + zu + Infinitiv

negative
meaning		 Du brauchst morgen nicht zu kommen. — You do not need to come tomorrow. = nicht
müssen
Du brauchst dir keine Sorgen zu machen. — You do not need to worry. = nicht
müssen
positive
meaning		 Ich brauche nur einmal pro Tag das Essen zu kochen. — I only need to cook food once a day. = müssen
Du brauchst bloß etwas zu sagen, dann helfe ich dir. — You only need to say something, then I will help you. = müssen

Note: Using the construction brauchen + zu + Infinitiv in a positive meaning by analogy with English “I need to do it” — “Ich brauche das zu machen”, i.e. without limiting words such as nur/bloß, is a mistake.
