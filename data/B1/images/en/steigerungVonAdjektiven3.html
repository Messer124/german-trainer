Adjectives in Superlativ have the form

adjective + (e)st + ending

and are used with the definite article or with article substitutes.

Compare:

Positiv:
Lea ist eine fleißige Schülerin. — Lea is a hardworking schoolgirl.
Komparativ:
Beate ist eine fleißigere Schülerin. — Beate is a more hardworking schoolgirl.
Superlativ:
Maria ist die fleißigste Schülerin. — Maria is the most hardworking schoolgirl.

Otherwise, adjectives in attributive Superlativ are declined in the same way as ordinary adjectives:

Kennst du den besten Schüler in unserer Schule? — Do you know the best student in our school?

Mein bester Freund heißt Mark. — My best friend is called Mark.
