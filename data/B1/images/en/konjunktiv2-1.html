The Konjunktiv II form for modal verbs in the past is formed with the Ersatzinfinitiv* by analogy with Perfekt and Plusquamperfekt:

Perfekt/Plusquamperfekt

Ich habe/hatte die Hausaufgabe machen müssen. — I had to do the homework.

Konjunktiv II

Ich hätte die Hausaufgabe machen müssen. — I should have done the homework. (But I did not)

The Konjunktiv II form for modal verbs in the past is very common in spoken German. Examples:

Du hättest es mir früher sagen sollen! — You should have told me that earlier!

Du hättest gehen sollen. — You should have left.

Ich hätte gestern arbeiten müssen. — I would have had to work yesterday.

Note: Ersatzinfinitiv is a German phenomenon in which the infinitive replaces Partizip II in Perfekt and Plusquamperfekt clauses with three or more verbs.

Example: Ich habe gestern meine Haare schneiden lassen. — I had my hair cut yesterday.
