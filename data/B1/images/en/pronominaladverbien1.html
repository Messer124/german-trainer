In German, you cannot simply say: “I am waiting the bus”.

The verb warten is used with the preposition auf:

Ich warte auf den Bus. — I am waiting for the bus.

To ask “What are you waiting for?” correctly, you need a pronominal adverb.

📖

Pronominal adverbs are helper words formed by joining the demonstrative pronoun da or the interrogative pronoun wo to a preposition.

◎ Reference
da+mit=damit
da+für=dafür
dar+auf=darauf
? Question
wo+mit=womit?
wo+für=wofür?
wor+auf=worauf?
i Before a vowel, r is added: darauf, darüber, worauf, worüber
