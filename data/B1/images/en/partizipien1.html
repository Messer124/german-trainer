A participle combines features of a verb and an adjective and is formed from a verb.

German has two types of participles:

das Partizip I or Partizip Präsens
das Partizip II or Partizip Perfekt

which are used to express:

active unfinished

activity

passive or completed active

activity

das malende Kind — the drawing child, i.e. the child who is drawing at the moment of speaking

das gemalte Bild — the painted picture, or a picture painted by someone (completed or unfinished passive activity)

der ankommende Zug — the arriving train, i.e. the train that is arriving at the moment of speaking

der angekommene Zug — the train that has arrived, i.e. the train that arrived by the moment of speaking (result of an active action)
