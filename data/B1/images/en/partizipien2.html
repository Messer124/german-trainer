Partizip I is formed from the infinitive of the verb by adding the suffix -d:

malen — malend

lachen — lachend

and is used:

1. Attributively (as an adjective)
Die lachende Frau beobachtet das Kind. — The laughing woman is watching the child.
Declined like an adjective
2. Adverbially
Die Frau verlässt lachend das Haus. — The woman leaves the house laughing.
Not declined; used rarely in spoken language
3. In participial phrases
Auf der Terrasse sitzend, aßen wir Kuchen und tranken Kaffee. — Sitting on the terrace, we ate cake and drank coffee.
Not declined; most often used in literary language
4. As a noun
Die Reisenden müssen selbst auf das Gepäck achten. — The travelers must look after their luggage themselves.
Declined like a substantivized adjective*
