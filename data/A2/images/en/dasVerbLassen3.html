The Perfekt forms of lassen in its main and auxiliary meanings:

As a main verb As an auxiliary verb
haben + gelassen haben + infinitive of the main verb + lassen
Ich habe mein Auto zu Hause gelassen. Ich habe mein Auto waschen lassen.

Important:

Regardless of its meaning, the verb lassen requires the accusative after it.

Lass deine Sachen hier. — Leave your things here.

Lass mich das Fenster aufmachen. — Let me open the window.

Lassen wir uns baden gehen! — Let's go swimming!
